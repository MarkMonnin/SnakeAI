About

This lab lets you train neural and tabular agents to play Snake, but the game is hard enough that clever algorithms are the practical way to go. Open Observe to watch baselines and AIs in training; use Shape Search / Train One if you want to keep pushing learners.

Full writeup and credits: README.md

Algorithms (in short)

Master
Even boards use Cell Tree; odd boards use Tail Safe Hybrid. It is the default Observe bot and topped the 4..11 x100 tournament.
Cell Tree
Drives clockwise in every 2x2 cell and crosses cells only on a spanning tree. Even boards use the full grid. Odd boards stay on Cell Tree Odd until fill reaches about 36%, then transitions to Hamilton.
Cell Tree Odd
Dedicated no-Hamilton odd algorithm (Cell Tree hybrid listed above falls back to Hamilton for odd-size boards). Even boards use full-board Cell Tree. About a 54% win rate on 11x11 boards.
DHCR / DHCR Improved
Keeps a Hamiltonian cycle and shortcuts only when the cycle can be rewritten to stay Hamiltonian. Improved handles when the repaired cycle loops (state repeat / starve), then returns to repair after the next apple.
Hamilton / Hamilton Aggressive
Follows a fixed Hamiltonian cycle with gated free-arc shortcuts. Aggressive takes more shortcuts early (under about 20% fill), then matches normal Hamilton.
Tail Safe / Tail Safe Hybrid
Ranks moves by tail-reachability and open space (tip treated empty on the next non-eat). Hybrid runs Tail Safe until a loop or ~20% fill, then transitions to Hamilton.
Plan Ahead
Plans a short path that stays in the largest opening, then enters Hamilton transition with a lookahead when stuck. It re-enters planning and does not hand off to Hamilton, though late-game can look similar to Hamilton because the transition method matches that.
Avoid Pocket / BFS / Greedy / Wall follower / Random
Classic heuristics: pocket-safe BFS, shortest path, Manhattan greedy, wall hug, or a random legal step. They move quickly early but rarely finish large boards alone.
